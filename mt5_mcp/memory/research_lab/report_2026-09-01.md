**Commits:** `ae1215d`, `172f596`, `19823f7`, `901bbf2`

## Executive summary

The accounting forensics invalidated every previously reported "win."
With a fully reconciled engine (trade log == equity, drift $0.00), all
4 symbols are net-negative (PF 0.58–0.90). The regime-gated reward
(v4) was 2-for-4 at beating baseline — directionally interesting on
GBPUSD/XAUUSD, harmful on EURUSD/USDJPY — and 0-for-4 at profitability.
**No config is promoted.** The dominant cost driver is churn: holding
times under 1 hour mean spread+commission consume any gross edge.

## Hypotheses tested

1. **Inaction penalty causes unprofitable forced trades** — partially
   true: gating changed WHERE trades happened but not profitability.
2. **Regime gating targets entry quality** — weakly supported (2/4
   improved) but the effect size (+0.03..+0.08 PF) is noise-level.

## Learning log (rejections)

- **Flat inaction penalty** — manufactures activity, not alpha.
- **Regime-gated reward as implemented** — inconsistent cross-symbol;
   rejected for promotion. Keep the mechanism (it's now a tool), but
   the entry-quality problem is not regime selection alone.
- **All v1–v3 PF/WR numbers** — invalid; produced by engines hiding
   SL/TP losses (5 accounting bugs, all fixed and reconciliation-tested).

## Root-cause analysis: cost churn

Per-trade costs: EURUSD spread 1.5p + slippage 1p + commission $7
≈ $10-12 per 0.01-lot round trip on a $50 risk budget — **20-25% of
risked capital per trade**. With avg hold < 1 hour and WR 35-45%, the
agent must overcome a ~25% cost headwind. Expectancy -$12.6 to -$12.9
per trade ≈ exactly the cost stack. **The strategies aren't losing to
the market; they're losing to the spread.**

## Recommended next steps (ranked)

1. **Attack holding time** (biggest lever): min-hold constraint (e.g.,
   no exit before 4 bars) or exit-penalty for <2-bar holds — forces
   the agent past noise-scale moves.
2. **Cost-aware reward**: subtract actual per-trade cost from reward
   at close so the agent internalizes churn.
3. **Longer TP / partial exits**: current 1.2-2.0 ATR SL vs 2.5-3.0
   ATR TP with <1h holds means TPs rarely fill; exits are mostly
   signal-flips eating spread.
4. If after cost fixes the honest baseline still shows PF<1 across
   the board, conclude PPO-per-symbol on H1 raw features has no edge
   and pivot to the (separately validated) supply/demand price-action
   strategy as the prop-side engine.
